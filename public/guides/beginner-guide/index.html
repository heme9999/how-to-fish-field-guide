How to Fish beginner guide

The opening loop is short: gather, sell, buy a rod, catch a creature, then defeat it on land. This route avoids wasting early cash.

Reviewed: August 27, 2026Scope: launch versions 1.0.xStatus: cross-checked

1. Gather and sell clams

Collect the clams available around the starting area. Selling these gives you the first small amount of cash needed to enter the fishing loop.

2. Buy the starter gear

Use your opening cash for the basic rod and beginner lure offered during the tutorial sequence. Avoid treating any launch price as permanent: early patches may rebalance the economy.

3. Cast, reel and land the catch

Equip the rod with a compatible lure, cast into open water and reel after a bite. Landing a creature does not complete the catch. It enters the physics-driven combat phase on shore.

4. Defeat the creature safely

Control the landing area before attacking. A catch can remain dangerous after it leaves the water, so keep space and avoid hazards before deciding whether to sell, cook or retain it for a quest.

5. Prepare before the first boss

Complete the sailor’s tutorial and build a small cash buffer before triggering the Spider Crab encounter. Losing may force you to repeat part of the preparation.
