How to beat Spider Crab

Do not rush this first boss with bare fists. Finish the tutorial, improve your close-range damage and enter with enough cash to absorb a failed attempt.

Reviewed: August 27, 2026Source level: guide-reviewedSpoilers: early game only

Preparation

  1. Finish the sailor’s tutorial sequence.
  2. Earn additional cash from early catches instead of triggering the fight immediately.
  3. Buy brass knuckles or another available damage upgrade before using the beer that leads into the encounter.

During the fight

Keep moving laterally and wait for the Spider Crab to finish its attack animation before committing to a short counterattack. Extended bare-fist trading is inefficient and makes the retry cost more likely.

If you lose

A failed attempt can require gathering enough money to obtain another beer and repeat the trigger. That makes preparation more valuable than an immediate first attempt.

Short answer: complete the tutorial, buy brass knuckles, avoid the crab’s attack, then punish its recovery rather than trading hits.
