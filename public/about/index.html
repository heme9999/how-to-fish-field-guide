Method, sources and corrections

This is an independent companion for the 2026 Steam game How to Fish. Its purpose is to make collection and progression information quicker to use, not to imitate the game or republish another guide.

Verification labels

  • In-game verified: reproduced directly in the stated build.
  • Official: supported by the developer, store page or patch notes.
  • Cross-checked: independently reported by at least two current sources.
  • Community-confirmed: plausible but awaiting direct reproduction.

Launch sources

The seed dataset was cross-checked against current coverage from PC Gamer, GamesRadar, Mobalytics Gamebase and other launch guides. Source claims are paraphrased, and uncertain prices or formulas are excluded.

Corrections

Every data page shows its review date. A public correction channel will be added before the database expands beyond the launch seed set.
